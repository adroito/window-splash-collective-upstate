Why a co-op? Fair pay is the point.

Businesses have hired an artist to paint a whole storefront and paid pocket change for it. Artists working alone get played against each other, and the price drops.

The co-op stops that. Members quote together and hold one fair price — the same standard for every artist, every job. The first members set it. Everyone who joins works to it.

No more huge jobs for $200.

Questions storefront owners ask

Window splash painting in Spartanburg and the Upstate.

What is a window splash?

Paint on a storefront window. Bold pictures and lettering, painted right on the glass to announce a season, a sale, or an opening. It’s the name for storefront window painting, and it’s what we do in Spartanburg and Upstate SC.

Is window splash art permanent?

No. It’s made to come off. It holds up outdoors while it’s up, then comes down clean when the next design goes on.

How is the price worked out?

By window size and design — how much glass, how much detail, when you need it. No flat rate card. The co-op’s artists set one fair standard, and your price is confirmed before anything is booked.

What kinds of businesses order it?

Restaurants, fast food, retail, dealerships, furniture stores, mom-and-pops, food trucks. Big and small. If people pass your front, painted glass talks to them.

What can be painted besides a sale?

Grand openings, holidays, logos, hand lettering, sign painting, murals, wall graphics. Glass, murals, anything custom — ask.

Where does the co-op work, and how fast?

Spartanburg and Upstate SC. Most jobs are quick. Put your date in the form below and we’ll match an artist to it.

Why book through a co-op instead of hiring direct?

Fair pay is built in. Members set prices together, so no artist is played against another and no job is priced under what the work is worth. You get one quote, confirmed up front. No more huge jobs for $200.

How do artists join the co-op?

Fill in the artist signup form on this page. Signing up does not commit you to any job — each match is confirmed with you first.
